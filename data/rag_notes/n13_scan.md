# Scanning tips and misread colors

*id: n13_scan - tag: vision*

Scan in even light and avoid glare on the stickers. The pairs most often confused are white and yellow under warm light, and orange and red on worn stickers. Hold each face flat and centered in the overlay, in the order the tutor asks. The tutor reads every sticker by comparing it to the six center stickers, because centers never move, and checks that each color appears exactly nine times. If a count is wrong, rescan that face or correct the sticker by hand in the interface.
