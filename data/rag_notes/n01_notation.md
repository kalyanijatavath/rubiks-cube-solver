# Move notation

*id: n01_notation - tag: notation*

Each letter names a face: U is the top (up) face, D bottom (down), F front, B back, L left and R right. A letter on its own means a quarter turn clockwise, as if you were looking straight at that face. A prime mark, as in R', means a quarter turn counterclockwise. A 2, as in R2, means a half turn; direction does not matter for a half turn. Read a sequence from left to right and finish each move before starting the next. The letters always refer to faces of the cube, never to your left or right hand.
