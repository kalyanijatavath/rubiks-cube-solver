# Stage 1: the bottom cross

*id: n05_cross - tag: stage1*

Goal: the four bottom edges each have the bottom color on the bottom face and their other sticker matches the center of the side face they touch. Matching the bottom color alone is not enough. Work on one edge at a time: find an edge that has the bottom color, turn the top layer until its other color sits above the matching side center, then turn that side face a half turn to drop the edge into place. Check all four side stickers against the centers at the end.
