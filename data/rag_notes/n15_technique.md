# Turning technique and speed

*id: n15_technique - tag: technique*

Learn each algorithm slowly and smoothly before trying to go fast; speed comes from consistency, not from rushing. Keep the cube in the same hand position, use the index and middle fingers to flick the top layer, and avoid regripping mid-algorithm. Say the move names quietly while practicing. Do not stop in the middle of an algorithm: if you lose your place, undo back to the start of the algorithm or ask the tutor to check the cube.
